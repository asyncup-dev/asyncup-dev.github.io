💬

One-tap standups

A DM card opens one form — yesterday, today, blockers, plus a private mood with an optional comment. Edit until the deadline; the posted card updates in place.

🧵

Tidy date threads

Every answer lands as one card per person under the day's thread. The wrap-up posts the count and exactly who's missing.

🏖️

Away-aware

Skip today with a one-line reason posted to the thread (and undo), vacation mode, personal weeks and days off, and Google Calendar OOO sync — away people are never nagged or counted as missing.

⚠️

Blockers that follow up

Blockers open from answers and can be worked as items — tag teammates, acknowledge, post updates, resolve — with daily nudges and stale-blocker escalation.

📊

Insights built in

Trend charts (participation, mood, blockers), weekly digests, anonymous team-mood mode, CSV export, polls, and signed outbound webhooks.

👥

Rosters that keep up

A People grid for who's on, who's required and which days each person works — or follow a Google Group so joiners and leavers sync themselves.

🖥️

Consoles with SSO

Workspace admins sign into the console; everyone else gets a personal page. Google sign-in or SAML, SCIM provisioning — all in the MIT core.

🤖

MCP for your own assistants

Claude Code and other MCP clients can read standups, answer or skip, work blockers, and nudge the team — scoped tokens, no model inside AsyncUp.

🪶

Lightweight forever

One ~300 MB container on 1 vCPU / 512 MB. Embedded SQLite by default — or bring your own PostgreSQL with one env var.

🔌

Platform-agnostic core

Google Chat today; Slack and Microsoft Teams adapters are next on the roadmap. MIT licensed, never SaaS.

How a morning works

No meeting, no interrogation bot — one card, one form, one thread.

1

The bot DMs your team

At 09:30 in each person's own timezone, everyone gets a card with a Fill standup button — yesterday pre-filled from their last "today". One gentle reminder before the deadline.

2

Answers thread up neatly

Each submission posts as a card under the day's thread in your team space. Re-submit to edit in place. Skips, vacations, and calendar OOO show as away — never as missing.

3

The wrap-up holds the line

At the deadline: who submitted, who didn't, who's away and why, open blockers — plus a weekly digest with trends. Moods stay private, in the web app.
